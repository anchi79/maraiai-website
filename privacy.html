2. Marai in short

Marai is an AI co-founder for small, founder-led businesses. With your permission, it reads your business email and documents and turns them into briefings, reminders, risks to watch and suggested next steps.

The short version of how we treat your data:

  • You decide what Marai can see. You connect your accounts yourself and can disconnect them at any time.
  • Marai never acts on its own. Sending an email, creating a task or any other action needs your tap first.
  • Your data is used only to provide Marai to you. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models.
  • Your data is kept separate. Each business has its own database. We do not learn from one customer's data to serve another.
  • You can leave cleanly. Ask us to delete your data and we will, and confirm when it is done.

You can disconnect Gmail or Microsoft 365 at any time in Marai's settings, or remove Marai's access from your Google or Microsoft account security page. When you disconnect, Marai stops reading new email straight away.

6. How Marai uses AI

Marai uses artificial intelligence to understand your emails and documents and to write briefings and suggestions. You are always talking to an AI, not a person, and Marai says so.

  • Code first, AI only when needed. Calculations, dates and figures are worked out by our own software. AI is used only where judgement or writing is needed.
  • Only what is needed. For each task, Marai sends the AI provider only the pieces of information that task needs, not your whole mailbox.
  • Some data never goes to AI. Highly sensitive items, such as passwords, bank login details and full card or account numbers, are never sent to AI models. Exact money figures are shared with AI only where you allow it in your settings.
  • No training on your data. Our AI providers process your data under commercial terms that do not allow them to train their models on it. We do not train AI models on your data either.
  • No learning across customers. During the beta, Marai does not use one business's data to improve answers for another.
  • Checked before you see it. Important figures in Marai's findings are checked against your records by our software before they are shown to you.

10. Security

We protect your data with measures suited to business email and financial information, including:

  • a separate database and database login for each business, so one customer's data cannot be reached from another's account;
  • encryption of data in transit (HTTPS) and at rest;
  • secrets and keys kept in a managed key vault, never in code;
  • access for our team only where needed, protected by multi-factor authentication and logged;
  • regular backups and tested restores.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we will tell you and the relevant authorities as the law requires.

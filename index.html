Questions founders ask

Who is Marai for?

Founder-led businesses that run on email: services and project businesses, consultancies, agencies and subscription businesses, anywhere in the world. Marai works in English.

What can I connect during the beta?

Gmail or Microsoft 365 email, with read-only access, plus spreadsheet imports of clients, suppliers and invoices. Subscription businesses can also connect Stripe, read-only.

Will Marai send emails or act without me?

No. Marai can read the email you connect but can’t send it. She prepares drafts and suggestions, and you decide what to do with them.

Is my data safe?

Each business has its own separate database, email access is read-only, and your data is never sold or used to train AI. You can disconnect or ask us to delete everything at any time. See the Privacy Notice for the details.

Can I see Marai before I join?

Yes. Every beta member gets a personal onboarding call, where we walk you through Marai before you connect anything.

Can my business partner use it too?

Yes. During the beta, the owner and one business partner can each use Marai, with their own private conversations.

What happens when the beta ends?

We’ll share pricing before the beta ends. You choose whether to continue. If you don’t, we delete your data and confirm it in writing.

Does Marai replace my accountant or adviser?

No. Marai helps you understand your business and decide faster. Accounting, tax and legal advice still belong with qualified professionals.
